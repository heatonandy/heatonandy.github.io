I grew up with an incredible family, the best friends you could ask for, and never feeling like I was every going to be good enough.  In my 20's while on my  mental health journey, I discovered that writing the mess that was in my mind helped me to think more clearly.  I discovered how theraputic and powerful writing could be in helping me organize my thoughts.  And as I get older, I seem to have more and more thoughts.  

For a long time I thought it would be fun to have a place where I could share my thoughts with others.  This is my first stab at that.
